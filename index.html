Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for families comparing childcare and preschool in Sterling, VA.

These are the practical questions most parents want answered before booking or attending a tour.

What ages do you serve?

Karter School of Cascades offers programs by age group, including infant care, toddler childcare, preschool, Pre-K, kindergarten, after-school programs, and summer camp. Use the tour request form to confirm current program fit for your child's age.

How do I check current availability?

The fastest way is to request a private tour and share your child's age group and desired start timing. Admissions can explain current openings by program and help you understand what timing may be realistic.

Can I ask about tuition during the tour?

Yes. The tour is a good time to ask about tuition, value, scheduling, and next enrollment steps so your family can compare options with more clarity.

Can I tour before enrolling?

Yes. You can book a private tour, meet the team, see classrooms, ask questions about safety and communication, and decide whether the campus feels like a fit.

What should I ask on the tour?

Ask about age fit, current openings, tuition next steps, daily routines, teacher communication, safety practices, classroom transitions, and what happens after the tour.

Is Karter close to families in Cascades or Loudoun?

Karter School of Cascades is located at 21290 Springlake Ct, Sterling, VA 20166, serving families in Sterling, Cascades, and nearby Loudoun communities.

What makes Karter different from daycare options nearby?

Karter combines age-based programs, parent communication, trusted reviews, campus tours, and a focus on safety, warmth, and early growth. Families can tour to compare fit before deciding.

Do you offer summer camp in Sterling, VA?

Karter School of Cascades includes seasonal summer camp programming. Families can ask about current camp availability, schedule, and next steps through the tour form.

What makes Karter's Montessori approach different?

Karter's Montessori approach is student-led and self-paced, while still guided by knowledgeable and caring teachers. Children learn through hands-on exploration, independence-building routines, and a nurturing classroom environment designed to support curiosity and confidence.

What do children learn in a Montessori classroom?

Karter's Montessori learning areas include practical life, sensorial exploration, mathematics, cultural studies, and language. These areas help children build independence, problem-solving, early academic skills, creativity, and a stronger love for learning.

Is Karter a Montessori school in Sterling, VA?
